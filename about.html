The In-Salon Progression

Three moments of the ritual.

Every appointment moves through three intentional stages designed for scalp renewal, cortex repair, and tactile styling.

First Movement

The Basin Experience

Lukewarm purified water and botanically derived clarifying cleansers. A methodical detoxing scalp massage stimulates microcirculation while relieving tension in the neck and temples.

Detoxing Basin Massage
Second Movement

Molecular Restoration

Targeted peptide formulations and concentrated Japanese hair masks. K18 bio-mimetic peptides reconnect broken keratin chains, reversing structural damage from bleach and thermal styling.
